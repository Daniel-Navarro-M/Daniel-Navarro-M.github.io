MA thesis · 2026CEU

(I'm)Paz Total: did Colombia's peace policy reduce violence?

A monthly panel built from National Police records and ACLED events for Buenaventura, Arauca and Tumaco, estimated with two-way fixed effects and event studies, plus robustness checks and QGIS maps.

  • Causal inference
  • TWFE
  • Event study
  • R
  • ACLED

Finding: the onset of peace talks is linked to more monthly violence, led by political violence.

Procurement analytics · 2025CEU

Corruption-risk analytics for Rail Baltica

With Paula Biša: a contract-level Corruption Risk Index from red-flag indicators validated by logistic regression, comparing the Latvian implementer of Rail Baltica with the rest of the national market on TED data, 2018 to 2023.

  • Red flags
  • Logistic regression
  • TED data
  • R

Finding: no sign of unusually high risk overall; the implementer sits within, and partly below, market norms.
